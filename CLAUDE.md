# Project instructions

## Grill before advising

When I present a plan, strategy, campaign, launch, pricing, hiring, process change, or any decision with real money, time, or people at stake, run the `grill-me` skill (`.claude/skills/grill-me/SKILL.md`) first. Interview me one question at a time, with your recommended answer for each, before giving your own recommendation or starting to build.

Do not grill when:
- I ask a simple factual question or a quick, well-defined task.
- I say "skip grill", "tanpa grill", or "lanjut aja".
- We are already mid-grill. Continue with the next question instead of restarting.

If a question can be answered from the files in this repo, read them instead of asking me.
